Remediation program tracker

A spreadsheet for tracking customer or member remediation programs from identification to closure: how many people are affected, how much is owed and paid, and whether the program is on time.

Excel spreadsheet Last reviewed: 28 September 2026
Download the template (.xlsx)

Excel workbook, about 20 KB. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice. Free to use and adapt.

Key takeaways

  • Remediation puts affected customers back in the position they would have been in, including interest or earnings for the time value of money.
  • Track both people and dollars: "% paid" by number of customers and by amount can tell different stories.
  • Plan early for customers you can't reach or pay. ASIC RG 277 sets out options for residual funds.
  • A program isn't finished until the root cause is fixed and payments are assured.

Key calculated measures

ColumnWhat it does
% customers paid / % of compensation paidProgress by people and by dollars.
Customers not yet paidAffected customers minus customers paid: the group that needs a residual plan.
Days openFrom date identified to closure, or to today if still open.
Overdue?OVERDUE if the target completion date has passed and the program isn't closed.

A starting point, not a finished framework

The tracker records your decisions. It doesn't decide who must be remediated or how much. Use RG 277, your policies and, for super trustees, the best financial interests duty, and seek advice where needed.

Regulatory obligation checklists

Quick self-checks for six sets of obligations that financial services organisations commonly manage. Tick what's in place here, or download the spreadsheet version to record evidence, owners and actions.

Intermediate Interactive checklists and Excel download Last reviewed: 27 September 2026
Download all checklists (.xlsx)

One tab per checklist, with status drop-downs, evidence, owner, action and due date columns, plus a summary tab. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice.

Key takeaways

  • Each item is a plain-English summary of a requirement or good practice, with a reference to where it comes from. It is not the legal text.
  • A tick should mean "we could show evidence of this", not just "we think we do this".
  • These checklists are for learning and first-pass self-assessment. A full compliance review needs your organisation's own obligations register and the current law and guidance.
  • Ticks on this page aren't saved. Use the spreadsheet to keep a record.

Checklists on this page

  • CPS 230 Operational risk management (18 items)
  • Breach reporting readiness (ASIC reportable situations) (13 items)
  • Complaints handling (IDR, RG 271) (11 items)
  • Design and distribution obligations (RG 274) (9 items)
  • Whistleblower program (Part 9.4AAA, RG 270) (11 items)
  • Privacy and data breach readiness (12 items)

CPS 230 Operational risk management

Applies to: APRA-regulated banks, insurers and super trustees (in force since 1 July 2025). Read the explainer

Breach reporting readiness (ASIC reportable situations)

Applies to: AFS licensees and Australian credit licensees, including super trustees. Read the explainer

Complaints handling (IDR, RG 271)

Applies to: AFS licensees and credit licensees serving retail clients, super trustees and other firms required to have IDR. Read the explainer

Design and distribution obligations (RG 274)

Applies to: Issuers and distributors of most retail financial products (MySuper products and some others are excluded). Read the explainer

Whistleblower program (Part 9.4AAA, RG 270)

Applies to: Public companies, large proprietary companies and super trustee companies must have a policy. The protections apply more widely. Read the explainer

Privacy and data breach readiness

Applies to: Organisations covered by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), generally including businesses with turnover over $3 million. Read the explainer

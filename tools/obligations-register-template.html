Obligations register template

A spreadsheet for recording what the organisation must comply with, where each obligation comes from, who owns it, which controls make sure it is met and when it was last reviewed.

Excel spreadsheet Last reviewed: 28 September 2026
Download the template (.xlsx)

Excel workbook, about 28 KB. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice. Free to use and adapt.

Key takeaways

  • An obligations register is the foundation of a compliance program: you can't monitor, test or report on obligations you haven't identified.
  • Write each obligation in plain English and record its exact source (section, paragraph or clause) so it can be checked and updated.
  • Every obligation needs an owner and linked controls. Residual risk depends on how well those controls work.
  • Review dates calculate automatically, so overdue reviews stand out on the dashboard.

How the calculated columns work

ColumnWhat it does
Residual compliance riskA simple illustrative rule: effective controls lower the inherent rating one level, ineffective controls raise it one level, and partially effective or untested controls leave it unchanged.
Next review dueLast reviewed date plus the review frequency in months.
Review statusOVERDUE, Due soon (within 30 days) or Current.

A starting point, not a finished framework

The register records obligations you have identified. It doesn't tell you which obligations apply to you. Build it from the legislation, instruments, standards, licence conditions and codes that apply to your organisation, and update it as laws change (the regulatory tracker can help). The examples are simplified and may not reflect every detail of the law.

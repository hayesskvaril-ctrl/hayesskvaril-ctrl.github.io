Material service provider register

A spreadsheet for managing material service provider arrangements under CPS 230: what each provider does, which critical operations rely on it, where the service and data are, and when contracts and reviews fall due.

Intermediate Excel spreadsheet Last reviewed: 28 September 2026
Download the template (.xlsx)

Excel workbook, about 19 KB. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice. Free to use and adapt.

Key takeaways

  • Under CPS 230, APRA-regulated entities must maintain a register of material service providers and give it to APRA.
  • APRA publishes its own register template, which it prefers for submissions. This workbook is for internal management alongside it.
  • Record where services are delivered and data is held, including offshore locations and key fourth parties.
  • Contract and review alerts calculate automatically, so upcoming renewals and overdue reviews stand out.

A starting point, not a finished framework

For submission to APRA, use APRA's official template and check its current instructions. Whether a provider is "material" depends on CPS 230 and your own assessment; see third-party and outsourcing risk. The 180-day and 12-month alert periods are illustrative defaults.

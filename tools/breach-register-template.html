Breach register template

A spreadsheet for logging breaches, recording why they are or aren't reportable, tracking regulator deadlines and following remediation through to closure, with a ready-made dashboard.

Intermediate Excel spreadsheet Last reviewed: 27 September 2026
Download the template (.xlsx)

Excel workbook, about 30 KB. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice. Free to use and adapt.

Key takeaways

  • A breach register is core evidence that an organisation identifies, assesses, reports and fixes breaches. Regulators and auditors often ask to see it.
  • Record the date aware for every entry. The template calculates the ASIC reportable situations deadline (30 calendar days) from it.
  • Record significance reasoning, especially for breaches you decide not to report.
  • Use the dashboard to spot trends, recurring root causes and overdue reports for management and board reporting.

How the deadline tracking works

ColumnWhat it does
ASIC deadline (30 days)If "ASIC report required?" is Yes, adds 30 calendar days to the date aware.
ASIC status / APRA statusShows Due in X days until lodged, OVERDUE if the deadline has passed without lodgement, then On time or Late once lodged.
APRA deadlineEntered by you, because APRA timeframes vary: 24 hours, 72 hours, 10 business days or 30 days depending on the requirement.
Days openDays from date aware to closure, or to today if still open.

A tool, not a legal test

The register tracks your decisions. It doesn't decide whether a breach is significant or reportable. Use RG 78, the breach reporting obligations page and your own policies, and seek advice where needed. Some reporting timeframes and ASIC relief can change, so check the current position.

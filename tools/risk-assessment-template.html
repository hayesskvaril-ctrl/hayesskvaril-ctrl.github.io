Risk assessment template

A ready-to-use risk register spreadsheet. Describe each risk, rate it before and after controls, check it against your appetite, and plan treatment. The heat maps update automatically.

Beginner Excel spreadsheet Last reviewed: 27 September 2026
Download the template (.xlsx)

Excel workbook, about 25 KB. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice. Free to use and adapt.

Key takeaways

  • Rate every risk twice: inherent (before controls) and residual (with current controls). The gap shows how much you rely on your controls.
  • Replace the example scales with the ones in your organisation's approved risk management framework, so ratings are consistent.
  • Set the appetite cell to match your risk appetite statement. Residual risks above it are flagged for treatment.
  • A rating is only as good as the evidence behind it. Record why you believe controls are effective, such as recent control testing.

Columns in the register

GroupColumnsTips
IdentifyRisk ID, title, description, category, ownerWrite the description as cause → event → consequence. Give every risk one named owner.
Inherent ratingLikelihood, consequence, score, ratingRate as if key controls weren't there. The score and rating calculate automatically.
ControlsKey controls, control type, effectivenessList the few controls that really matter. Mark "Not tested" if there's no recent evidence.
Residual ratingLikelihood, consequence, score, rating, within appetite?Rate with controls working as they do today, not as intended.

Worked example (included in the template)

FieldExample entry
RiskFee calculation error. Cause: product or system changes not tested for fee impacts. Event: fees calculated incorrectly. Consequence: members overcharged, remediation cost, reportable breach.
InherentLikely (4) × Moderate (3) = 12, High
Key controlsFee scenarios in change testing; monthly independent fee reconciliation; administrator SLA reporting (partially effective)
ResidualUnlikely (2) × Moderate (3) = 6, Medium: within the default appetite
TreatmentReduce: automate the fee reconciliation, and add fee sign-off to the product change process. Target: Rare (1) × Moderate (3) = 3, Low.

Adapt before you rely on it

The scales, dollar bands and appetite setting are examples only. APRA-regulated entities must follow their own board-approved risk management framework (CPS 220 / SPS 220). Use this template as a learning aid or a starting point, and check that it fits your framework.

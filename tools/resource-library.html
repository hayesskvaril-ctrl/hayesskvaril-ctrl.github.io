Good to know

  • Downloads are Excel and Word files that work in Microsoft Office, Google Sheets and Docs, and LibreOffice. They are free to use and adapt within your organisation.
  • Interactive tools and self-checks run in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
  • Videos are short, silent explainers with captions and full transcripts. You can download them for training.
  • Everything here is a learning aid or starting point, not advice. Adapt it to your organisation's own frameworks and obligations. See the full disclaimer.

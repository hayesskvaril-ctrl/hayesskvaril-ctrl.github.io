Tools and templates

Free, practical templates you can download and adapt: spreadsheets with working formulas, Word documents and checklists. No sign-up is needed.

Last reviewed: 27 September 2026

Before you use a template

  • These are starting points for learning and adaptation, not finished compliance documents. Tailor them to your organisation's size, licences and approved frameworks.
  • Spreadsheets work in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice. Word templates open in Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice.
  • Each template page explains how to use it and links to the topic pages behind it.

Resource library

Every template, interactive tool, self-check and learning resource on the site, in one filterable list.

Risk assessment template

A risk register spreadsheet with inherent and residual ratings, appetite checks and automatic heat maps.

Incident report template

A Word template for recording, assessing and escalating an incident, including breach and notification checks.

Breach register template

A spreadsheet for logging breaches, assessing reportability and tracking deadlines and remediation.

Regulatory obligation checklists

Interactive self-check checklists for key obligations, such as CPS 230, breach reporting and complaints handling.

Obligations register template

A register of compliance obligations, owners and controls, with residual risk and review tracking.

Control testing workpaper

Plan, perform and conclude a control test, with sample results and an automatic upper deviation limit.

RCSA template

Business-led assessment of risks and controls, with scores, appetite check and second-line challenge.

KRI library

20 example key risk indicators with thresholds and automatic Green/Amber/Red status.

Example risk appetite statement

A fictional super fund's risk appetite statement, with metrics, triggers and limits for each material risk.

Board risk report template

A CRO report to the board risk committee, with guidance for each section.

Material service provider register

Track material service providers, critical operations, locations, contracts and reviews under CPS 230.

Remediation program tracker

Track remediation programs: customers affected, compensation owed and paid, residual approach and closure.

Risk heat map tool

Score risks in your browser and see them on a heat map, then download as CSV.

RCSA template

A spreadsheet for a business unit to assess its own risks and controls, with space for the second line to record its challenge.

Intermediate Excel spreadsheet Last reviewed: 28 September 2026
Download the template (.xlsx)

Excel workbook, about 26 KB. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice. Free to use and adapt.

Key takeaways

  • An RCSA asks the people who run a process to identify its risks, rate them, and assess how well the controls work.
  • Write risks as cause, event and consequence, so controls can be matched to causes.
  • The overall control rating is the weaker of design and operating effectiveness: a well-designed control that isn't performed doesn't reduce risk.
  • Self-assessment is prone to optimism. Record second-line challenge and test key controls independently.

Scoring used in the template

Score = likelihood (1–5) × consequence (1–5)
ScoreRating
1 to 4Low
5 to 9Medium
10 to 14High
15 to 25Extreme

These bands are illustrative. Use your organisation's own risk matrix, and see risk assessment methodologies for the known weaknesses of scoring matrices.

A starting point, not a finished framework

The template is a simple, common RCSA layout. Your risk management framework decides the rating scales, the appetite rules and who must challenge and approve the results.

About RiskLens Australia

A free, independent library explaining Australian risk management, compliance and governance, from first principles to practitioner detail.

Beginner Last reviewed: 27 September 2026

In brief

  • Free for everyone: no login, no paywall, no sign-up.
  • Independent and non-commercial. It is not affiliated with any regulator, employer, industry body or training provider.
  • Built on primary sources (legislation and regulator guidance), written in plain English, with every page dated and sourced.
  • General education only, never advice. See the full disclaimer below.

Why this site exists

Risk, compliance and governance knowledge in Australia is spread across legislation, regulator websites, standards that cost money to read, and paid courses. RiskLens Australia brings the essentials together in one free place. It explains what the rules are, why they exist and how organisations put them into practice, with diagrams, worked examples, interactive tools and templates.

The site is for anyone who needs to understand this field: people starting out, professionals moving between industries, board members, students and experienced practitioners looking for a quick refresher.

Who is behind it

RiskLens Australia was created by an Australian risk and compliance professional working in the superannuation industry. Their experience includes incident and breach management, remediation, disclosure reviews, CPS 230 and operational risk, control design and testing, fee governance and third-party risk. The site is a personal, non-commercial project. Its content doesn't represent the views of any employer.

How content is made

PrincipleWhat it means in practice
Primary sources firstPages are built from legislation, prudential standards, regulatory guides and regulator publications, plus peer-reviewed research where relevant. Each page lists its sources with links to the official versions.
Checked, dated and labelledSpecific requirements, thresholds and dates are checked against official or reputable sources at the time of writing. Every page shows a "Last reviewed" date. Proposals are labelled as proposals, and uncertain or contested points say so.
Our own wordsStandards such as ISO 31000, ISO/IEC 27001 and the COSO frameworks are copyrighted. We explain them in our own words and don't reproduce their text or figures. Legislation and regulator guidance are summarised in plain English, with links to the source.
AI-assisted, human-reviewedPages are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools, then checked against the sources listed on each page. Pages are progressively reviewed by the site's creator, starting with their specialist areas.
No invented factsWe don't make up citations, cases, statistics or research. If something can't be verified, it is left out or clearly flagged.
LevelsPages are tagged Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced by where they start. Learning pathways suggest a reading order.

Corrections and feedback

Spotted an error, an out-of-date requirement or a broken link? Please report it by opening an issue on the site's public GitHub page. It needs a free GitHub account. Say which page, what's wrong and, if you can, the official source. Corrections are made as quickly as possible, and the page's "Last reviewed" date is updated.

Privacy

  • There are no accounts, forms, cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising on this site.
  • Interactive tools, such as quizzes, the risk heat map, pathways and flashcards, save your progress only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to us, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Using our content and templates

You are welcome to read, share and link to any page. The downloadable templates and checklists are free to use and adapt within your own organisation. If you share the site's content more widely, please credit RiskLens Australia and link back to the page. Material quoted or linked from regulators, legislation and other publishers remains subject to their own copyright and terms.

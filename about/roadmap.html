Site roadmap

What we're improving, stage by stage: what's done, what's in progress and what's next.

Site upgrade, October 2026 Last updated: 1 October 2026

8 of 10 stages done: now working on expert review of specialist pages.

Why we're upgrading

An outside review of the site found it broad and polished, but harder than it should be to find your way around, too anonymous to fully trust, and organised like a textbook rather than around the work people actually do. This upgrade fixes that, in stages. Each stage goes live as soon as it is ready.

The stages

  1. ✓

    Done Stage 1 of 10 · Finished 1 October 2026

    Trust: review labels and corrections

    Every article now shows whether it has been checked against its sources or expert reviewed, and every page has a link to suggest a correction. See it

  2. ✓

    Done Stage 2 of 10 · Finished 1 October 2026

    Practitioner playbooks

    Step-by-step guides with checklists for real situations: responding to an incident, assessing a breach, running a remediation, onboarding a service provider, reviewing disclosure, testing a control and your first 90 days. See it

  3. ✓

    Done Stage 3 of 10 · Finished 1 October 2026

    Obligations library

    117 plain-English obligation summaries from 37 regimes, searchable, filterable by organisation type and downloadable. See it

  4. ✓

    Done Stage 4 of 10 · Finished 1 October 2026

    Courses with progress

    Learning pathways now work like courses: mark pages as read, follow the next step, and print a record of learning. See it

  5. ✓

    Done Stage 5 of 10 · Finished 1 October 2026

    A simpler menu and home page

    A shorter menu, a single Topics page, a home page that asks who you are and points you to the right place, a what's-new strip and this roadmap. See it

  6. 6

    In progress Stage 6 of 10

    Expert review of specialist pages

    The site's creator, a risk and compliance professional, reviews the most-used specialist pages (CPS 230, breach reporting, incidents, remediation, controls, third-party risk and super) and signs them off. Related page

  7. ✓

    Done Stage 7 of 10 · Finished 1 October 2026

    Board briefings

    Printable two-page briefings on CPS 230, FAR, breach reporting, CPS 234 and AML/CTF, written for directors and executives. See it

  8. ✓

    Done Stage 8 of 10 · Finished 1 October 2026

    Automatic checks of outside links

    A monthly automated check that links to regulators, legislation and research still work, so broken source links are found and fixed quickly.

  9. ✓

    Done Stage 9 of 10 · Finished 1 October 2026

    Content tidy-up and regular news

    A check of every article for overlap found no duplicates, so guides now link directly to their playbook and board briefing instead. News moves to a monthly regulatory roundup, published in the first week of each month. See it

  10. 10

    Later Stage 10 of 10

    Obligations library, stage 2

    Breaking each obligation summary into the individual obligations in the law, with citations. Related page

Being considered

  • Email updates for people who prefer them to the RSS feed.
  • A dedicated web address for the site.

Have an idea, or found something that needs fixing? Use the Suggest a correction link at the bottom of any page. Finished changes are listed on What's new.

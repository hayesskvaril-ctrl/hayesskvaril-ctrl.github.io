Governance

How organisations are directed, controlled and held to account, with a focus on Australian boards, accountability and culture in financial services.

Beginner to Intermediate 18 topics Last reviewed: 27 September 2026 Checked against sources

New to governance?

Start with What is governance? and The Three Lines model, then follow the learning path. Terms are defined in the Glossary.

Changes under way

APRA is consolidating its governance, fit and proper and (for super) conflicts standards into a new CPS 510, released as a draft in June 2026, with a final version expected by the end of 2026 and commencement planned for January 2028. The pages in this section describe the current rules and flag the proposed changes.

Learning path

  1. Board structure and accountability

    Who sits on boards, what directors must do, committees and how accountability flows.

  2. Financial Accountability Regime (FAR)

    Named accountability for the most senior people in banks, insurers and super funds.

  3. Conflicts of interest

    Spotting and managing situations where interests pull in different directions.

  4. Remuneration governance

    How pay is designed and overseen so that it rewards the right behaviour.

  5. Whistleblower protections

    Who is protected, for what, and what organisations must have in place.

  6. Culture and conduct risk

    Why culture matters, how to assess it, and what regulators look for.

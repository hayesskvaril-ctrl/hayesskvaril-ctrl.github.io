Regulatory changes tracker

Recent and upcoming changes that matter for risk and compliance in Australian financial services, in one place, with the key dates and a link to the official source.

30 items Position as at 27 September 2026

How to use the tracker

  • Filter by regulator, status or sector. Each title links to a RiskLens explainer, and the last column links to the official source.
  • Status shows whether a change is in force, finalised but not yet started, still proposed, under review, or a report or guidance that signals regulator expectations.
  • Dates and proposals change. The tracker shows the position as at 27 September 2026. Always confirm with the official source before relying on it.

ChangeRegulatorStatusKey datesSectorsOfficial source
CPS 190 Recovery and Exit Planning
Plans for responding to severe financial stress and for an orderly exit if needed.		 APRA In force ADIs and insurers since 1 January 2024; RSE licensees since 1 January 2025 Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: CPS 190
CPS 230 Operational Risk Management
Cross-industry standard for operational risk, business continuity and service provider management.		 APRA In force Since 1 July 2025 Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: CPS 230
CPS 230 targeted amendments
Limited exemptions from some contract requirements for certain non-traditional service providers, plus updates to guidance and the material service provider register template.		 APRA In force Finalised 30 April 2026; effective 1 July 2026 Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: final targeted amendments
Debt-to-income limit on home lending
No more than 20% of an ADI's new mortgage lending may be at a debt-to-income ratio of six or more (measured separately for owner-occupier and investor lending).		 APRA In force From February 2026 Banking APRA: DTI limits
SPS 515 Strategic Planning and Member Outcomes (strengthened)
Links strategy and business planning to member outcomes, with annual business performance reviews.		 APRA In force Since 1 July 2025 Super APRA: SPS 515
Financial Accountability Regime for insurers and super
Accountable persons, accountability statements and maps, deferred remuneration and consequences for failures.		 APRA and ASIC In force Since 15 March 2025 (ADIs since 15 March 2024) Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: Financial Accountability Regime
Reportable situations relief (investigation period)
ASIC relief extends the period before an investigation into a possible significant breach becomes reportable from 30 to 60 days, among other relief.		 ASIC In force Since 27 June 2025 AFS licensees, Super, Banking, Insurance, Managed funds ASIC: further relief under the reportable situations regime
Mandatory climate-related financial disclosures
Sustainability reports with climate statements under AASB S2, phased in by size, with assurance ramping up to reasonable assurance for years starting 1 July 2030.		 ASIC and Treasury In force Group 1 from years starting 1 January 2025; Group 2 (including $5b+ super funds and schemes) from 1 July 2026; Group 3 from 1 July 2027 Listed entities, Super, Managed funds, Banking, Insurance ASIC: RG 280 Sustainability reporting
AML/CTF reforms
Modernised obligations for existing reporting entities, and the regime extended to lawyers, accountants, real estate professionals and others.		 AUSTRAC In force Existing reporting entities from 31 March 2026; tranche 2 sectors from 1 July 2026 All sectors AUSTRAC
Failure to prevent foreign bribery offence
Companies are criminally liable if an associate bribes a foreign public official for their benefit, unless they had adequate procedures to prevent it.		 Attorney-General's Department In force Since 8 September 2024 All sectors AGD: Foreign bribery
Payday Super
Employers pay super with wages; contributions must generally reach the fund within seven business days of payday.		 Government and ATO In force Since 1 July 2026 Super APRA: Payday Super readiness
Removal of card surcharges and lower interchange fees
Surcharging removed on eftpos, Mastercard and Visa card payments, with lower interchange fee caps and more fee transparency.		 RBA In force Most changes from 1 October 2026; foreign card interchange cap and some transparency measures from 1 April 2027 Banking, All sectors RBA: conclusions paper
Consumer Data Right: non-bank lenders
Non-bank lenders join the Consumer Data Right, allowing customers to share their data with accredited recipients.		 Treasury and ACCC In force Product data from 13 July 2026; consumer data from 9 November 2026 (initial providers) AFS licensees CDR: non-bank lenders sector
Scams Prevention Framework
Banks, telcos and digital platforms must prevent, detect, disrupt, report and respond to scams, with ASIC overseeing banks.		 ACCC and ASIC Finalised, starts soon Sectors designated May 2026; AFCA scam complaints from 1 July 2026; rules from 1 September 2026; most obligations expected from March 2027 Banking ACCC: Scams Prevention Framework
Phase-out of Additional Tier 1 capital
Bank hybrid capital (AT1) removed from the capital framework, replaced mainly by Common Equity Tier 1 and Tier 2 capital.		 APRA Finalised, starts soon From 1 January 2027; existing instruments expected to be gone by 2032 Banking APRA: AT1 phase-out finalised
Privacy Act reforms (first tranche)
New right to sue for serious invasions of privacy, transparency duties for automated decisions, and a children's privacy code. Further reform is expected.		 OAIC Finalised, starts soon Statutory tort in force since 10 June 2025; automated decision-making transparency from 10 December 2026; Children's Online Privacy Code by 10 December 2026 All sectors OAIC
Digital assets framework
Operators of digital asset platforms and tokenised custody platforms must hold an AFSL and meet platform-specific standards.		 Treasury and ASIC Finalised, starts soon Law since April 2026; implementation and ASIC standards to follow AFS licensees Parliament: Digital Assets Framework Bill
Draft CPS 510 Governance (consolidated)
One cross-industry standard replacing CPS/SPS 510, CPS/SPS 520 and SPS 521. Proposals include a 12-year default tenure limit for non-executive directors and a minimum of five directors for super trustees.		 APRA Proposed or consultation Consultation closed 28 August 2026; final expected Q4 2026; to apply from early 2028 Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: proposed changes to governance
Financial Accountability Regime simplification
Proposals to provide accountability statements and maps on request only, allow more time to register accountable persons, remove key functions requirements and raise notification thresholds.		 APRA and ASIC Proposed or consultation Announced June 2026; ASIC responsible manager evidence changes from October 2026; some changes need legislation Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: FAR changes to reduce burden
ASX Corporate Governance Principles, 5th edition
Update to the principles and recommendations that listed entities report against on an "if not, why not" basis.		 ASX Proposed or consultation Consultation draft 21 July 2026 (submissions closed 14 September 2026); reporting expected from financial years ending 30 June or 31 December 2028 Listed entities ASX: Corporate Governance Principles and Recommendations
Modern slavery: failure to prevent offence and civil penalties
A proposed criminal offence for companies with revenue over $100 million that fail to prevent modern slavery in their supply chains (with a reasonable steps defence), plus civil penalties for reporting failures.		 Attorney-General's Department Proposed or consultation Announced 16 July 2026; consultation closed 25 September 2026; legislation and timing to be confirmed, with a 12 to 18 month implementation period proposed All sectors Anti-Slavery Commissioner: Government's move to strengthen laws
General Insurance Code of Practice redraft
Redrafted code proposed to be enforceable in consumer contracts, with new commitments such as automatic acceptance of some home and motor claims left undecided after 12 months.		 Insurance Council of Australia Proposed or consultation Lodgement with ASIC expected late October 2026; about 24 months' transition after approval Insurance ICA: Code of Practice
Delivering Better Financial Outcomes: further advice reforms
Proposals include replacing Statements of Advice with advice records and clarifying advice super funds can provide; changes to the best interests duty and a new class of adviser remain uncertain.		 Treasury Proposed or consultation Tranche 1 in force since 10 January 2025; further tranches announced in 2026, timing not yet settled AFS licensees, Super ASIC: DBFO package
Payments licensing reforms (Tranche 1)
Brings payment service providers into the AFS licensing regime, with APRA prudential supervision of large stored value facilities.		 Treasury Proposed or consultation Draft legislation March 2026; proposed start 12 months after Royal Assent Banking, AFS licensees Treasury: payments licensing reforms
Life Insurance Code of Practice review
Independent review of the Life Code, conducted in a way intended to support a possible application for ASIC approval.		 Council of Australian Life Insurers Under review Independent review 2025–26; final report due mid-2026 Insurance Life Code review
Super performance test review
Options to strengthen the annual performance test, including treatment of assets without a suitable market benchmark.		 Treasury Under review Consultation ran 7 May to 19 June 2026; outcome pending Super Treasury consultation
Letter to industry on artificial intelligence
APRA called for a step-change in AI-related risk management, with expectations on cyber and information security, governance, supplier risk, and change management and assurance, including for AI agents.		 APRA Report or guidance Published 30 April 2026 Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: step-change in AI-related risk management
RG 181 Managing conflicts of interest (reissued)
ASIC's first major update to its conflicts guidance for AFS licensees since 2004, signalling higher expectations.		 ASIC Report or guidance Reissued 16 December 2025 AFS licensees, Super, Banking, Insurance, Managed funds ASIC: RG 181
Report 820: private credit surveillance
Found weaker practices in conflicts, fees and margins, valuation and transparency, and terminology across private credit funds.		 ASIC Report or guidance Published 5 November 2025; surveillance continuing in 2026 Managed funds ASIC: REP 820
Report 833: platform trustees' monitoring of risks to retirement savings
Review of six platform trustees found persistent gaps in monitoring advice fee deductions, unusual fees and investments, and high-risk switching.		 ASIC Report or guidance Published 29 June 2026 Super ASIC: 26-135MR

Regulatory changes tracker

Recent and upcoming changes that matter for risk and compliance in Australian financial services, in one place, with the key dates and a link to the official source.

41 items Position as at 8 October 2026

How to use the tracker

  • Filter by regulator, status or sector. Each title links to a RiskLens explainer, and the last column links to the official source.
  • Status shows whether a change is in force, finalised but not yet started, still proposed, under review, or a report or guidance that signals regulator expectations.
  • Dates and proposals change. The tracker shows the position as at 8 October 2026. Always confirm with the official source before relying on it.

ChangeRegulatorStatusKey datesSectorsOfficial source
CPS 190 Recovery and Exit Planning
Plans for responding to severe financial stress and for an orderly exit if needed.		 APRA In force ADIs and insurers since 1 January 2024; RSE licensees since 1 January 2025 Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: CPS 190
CPS 230 Operational Risk Management
Cross-industry standard for operational risk, business continuity and service provider management.		 APRA In force Since 1 July 2025 Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: CPS 230
CPS 230 targeted amendments
Limited exemptions from some contract requirements for certain non-traditional service providers, plus updates to guidance and the material service provider register template.		 APRA In force Finalised 30 April 2026; effective 1 July 2026 Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: final targeted amendments
Debt-to-income limit on home lending
No more than 20% of an ADI's new mortgage lending may be at a debt-to-income ratio of six or more (measured separately for owner-occupier and investor lending).		 APRA In force Since 1 February 2026 Banking APRA: DTI limits
SPS 515 Strategic Planning and Member Outcomes (strengthened)
Links strategy and business planning to member outcomes, with annual business performance reviews.		 APRA In force Since 1 July 2025 Super APRA: SPS 515
Three-tier proportionality for banks
Banks with total assets above $30 billion are significant financial institutions (up from $20 billion), banks above $300 billion form a new top tier, and a bank moving up a tier gets 12 months to adjust. Definitions are in CPS 001.		 APRA In force Confirmed 27 May 2026; in force since 1 July 2026 Banking APRA: three-tiered approach to proportionality in banking
Financial Accountability Regime for insurers and super
Accountable persons, accountability statements and maps, deferred remuneration and consequences for failures.		 APRA and ASIC In force Since 15 March 2025 (ADIs since 15 March 2024) Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: Financial Accountability Regime
Reportable situations relief (investigation period)
ASIC relief extends the period before an investigation into a possible significant breach becomes reportable from 30 to 60 days, among other relief.		 ASIC In force Since 27 June 2025 AFS licensees, Super, Banking, Insurance, Managed funds ASIC: further relief under the reportable situations regime
Mandatory climate-related financial disclosures
Sustainability reports with climate statements under AASB S2, phased in by size, with assurance ramping up to reasonable assurance for years starting 1 July 2030.		 ASIC and Treasury In force Group 1 from years starting 1 January 2025; Group 2 (including $5b+ super funds and schemes) from 1 July 2026; Group 3 from 1 July 2027 Listed entities, Super, Managed funds, Banking, Insurance ASIC: RG 280 Sustainability reporting
AML/CTF reforms
Modernised obligations for existing reporting entities, and the regime extended to lawyers, accountants, real estate professionals and others. AUSTRAC has been contacting, and in September 2026 began issuing infringement notices to, businesses that should have enrolled but hadn't.		 AUSTRAC In force Existing reporting entities from 31 March 2026; tranche 2 sectors from 1 July 2026 All sectors AUSTRAC
Failure to prevent foreign bribery offence
Companies are criminally liable if an associate bribes a foreign public official for their benefit, unless they had adequate procedures to prevent it.		 Attorney-General's Department In force Since 8 September 2024 All sectors AGD: Foreign bribery
Payday Super
Employers pay super with wages; contributions must generally reach the fund within seven business days of payday.		 Government and ATO In force Since 1 July 2026 Super APRA: Payday Super readiness
Removal of card surcharges and lower interchange fees
Surcharging removed on eftpos, Mastercard and Visa card payments, with lower interchange fee caps and more fee transparency.		 RBA In force Most changes since 1 October 2026; foreign card interchange cap and some transparency measures from 1 April 2027 Banking, All sectors RBA: conclusions paper
Consumer Data Right: non-bank lenders
Non-bank lenders join the Consumer Data Right, allowing customers to share their data with accredited recipients.		 Treasury and ACCC In force Product data from 13 July 2026; consumer data from 9 November 2026 (initial providers) AFS licensees CDR: non-bank lenders sector
Scams Prevention Framework
Banks, telcos and digital platforms must prevent, detect, disrupt, report and respond to scams, with ASIC overseeing banks.		 ACCC and ASIC Finalised, starts soon Sectors designated May 2026; AFCA membership required for regulated services from 1 September 2026; most obligations from 31 March 2027 (codes start on the later of that date and registration) Banking ACCC: Scams Prevention Framework
Phase-out of Additional Tier 1 capital
Bank hybrid capital (AT1) removed from the capital framework, replaced mainly by Common Equity Tier 1 and Tier 2 capital.		 APRA Finalised, starts soon From 1 January 2027; existing instruments expected to be gone by 2032 Banking APRA: AT1 phase-out finalised
Privacy Act reforms (first tranche)
New right to sue for serious invasions of privacy, transparency duties for automated decisions, and a children's privacy code. Further reform is expected.		 OAIC Finalised, starts soon Statutory tort in force since 10 June 2025; automated decision-making transparency from 10 December 2026; Children's Online Privacy Code by 10 December 2026 All sectors OAIC
Digital assets framework
Operators of digital asset platforms and tokenised custody platforms must hold an AFSL and meet platform-specific standards.		 Treasury and ASIC Finalised, starts soon Law since April 2026; implementation and ASIC standards to follow AFS licensees Parliament: Digital Assets Framework Bill
Draft CPS 510 Governance (consolidated)
One cross-industry standard replacing CPS/SPS 510, CPS/SPS 520 and SPS 521. Proposals include a 12-year default tenure limit for non-executive directors and a minimum of five directors for super trustees.		 APRA Proposed or consultation Consultation closed 28 August 2026; final expected Q4 2026; to apply from early 2028 Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: proposed changes to governance
Lower standardised credit risk weights for banks
Lower risk weights for some lending, including large public infrastructure, high-quality unrated corporates and residential land and construction lending. Part of a staged roadmap that also covers liquidity and market risk.		 APRA Proposed or consultation Consultation opened 29 June 2026; APRA aims to finalise in the second half of 2026; proposed start 1 April 2027 Banking APRA: changes to bank risk weights
Strengthening super investment governance (draft SPS 530)
Member-level limits on concentrated holdings in higher-risk investments (within an APRA ceiling), stronger management of investment-related conflicts, resources matched to the investment menu, and codified expectations on onboarding, monitoring, remediation, valuations and accountability. Expected to affect platform trustees most.		 APRA Proposed or consultation Released 30 September 2026; submissions close 3 February 2027; final expected first half of 2027; proposed start 1 January 2028 Super APRA: proposals to strengthen trustee investment governance
Financial Accountability Regime simplification
Proposals to remove the key functions requirements, drop direct reports from some accountability maps, raise notification thresholds, provide statements and maps on request only, and allow more time to register accountable persons.		 APRA and ASIC Proposed or consultation Announced 16 June 2026; consultation on the first rule changes ran 2 September to 2 October 2026, with changes expected to take effect in early 2027; ASIC responsible manager evidence changes from October 2026; some changes need legislation Banking, Insurance, Super ASIC: 26-204MR consultation on FAR streamlining
ASX Corporate Governance Principles, 5th edition
Update to the principles and recommendations that listed entities report against on an "if not, why not" basis. Keeps the eight principles, simplifies some areas and strengthens expectations on culture oversight.		 ASX Proposed or consultation Consultation draft 21 July 2026 (submissions closed 14 September 2026); recommendation to the ASX board expected by the end of 2026; proposed to apply from financial years starting 1 July 2027 Listed entities ASX: Corporate Governance Principles and Recommendations
Modern slavery: failure to prevent offence and civil penalties
A proposed criminal offence for companies with revenue over $100 million that fail to prevent modern slavery in their supply chains (with a reasonable steps defence), plus civil penalties for reporting failures.		 Attorney-General's Department Proposed or consultation Announced 16 July 2026; consultation closed 25 September 2026; legislation and timing to be confirmed, with a 12 to 18 month implementation period proposed All sectors Anti-Slavery Commissioner: Government's move to strengthen laws
Privacy Act reforms (second tranche)
Draft Privacy Amendment (Personal Data Protection) Bill 2026, centred on a requirement that handling personal information be lawful, fair and reasonable, with consent becoming one factor rather than a gateway in many cases. It would also require a statement to the Privacy Commissioner within 72 hours of believing there has been an eligible data breach.		 Attorney-General's Department Proposed or consultation Exposure draft released 31 August 2026; consultation closed 18 September 2026; bill planned for introduction by December 2026 All sectors AGD: privacy reform consultation
General Insurance Code of Practice redraft
Redrafted code proposed to be enforceable in consumer contracts, with new commitments such as automatic acceptance of some home and motor claims left undecided after 12 months.		 Insurance Council of Australia Proposed or consultation Lodgement with ASIC now targeted for late October 2026 (delayed twice); about 24 months' transition after ASIC approval Insurance ICA: Code of Practice
CSLR 2026–27 special levy
About $190.3 million of the estimate relates to financial advice, well above the $20 million annual cap for advice licensees. Treasury proposed spreading the excess across advice licensees, responsible entities of managed investment schemes and other retail-facing sub-sectors.		 Treasury Proposed or consultation Revised levy estimate $198.1 million; consultation on sharing the special levy closed in early October 2026; decision pending AFS licensees, Managed funds, Super Treasury: CSLR 2026–27 special levy
Delivering Better Financial Outcomes: further advice reforms
Targeted changes to the best interests duty to enable scaled advice, streamlined statements of advice, a review of the Code of Ethics, and a new class of adviser initially limited to APRA-regulated super funds and life insurers for three years.		 Treasury Proposed or consultation Tranche 1 in force since 10 January 2025; remaining reforms confirmed 19 August 2026; legislation not yet passed AFS licensees, Super ASIC: DBFO package
Payments licensing reforms (Tranche 1)
Brings payment service providers into the AFS licensing regime, with APRA prudential supervision of large stored value facilities.		 Treasury Proposed or consultation Exposure draft March 2026 (consultation closed April 2026); Treasury intends to introduce a bill in 2026; proposed start 12 months after Royal Assent Banking, AFS licensees Treasury: payments licensing reforms
Evolution of the Essential Eight
The Essential Eight would become the first chapter, Essentials for enterprise IT, of a new Essentials series based on the Information Security Manual.		 Australian Signals Directorate Under review Consultation closed 12 July 2026; the November 2023 maturity model still applies; no retirement date confirmed All sectors ASD: consultation on evolution of Essential Eight
Life Insurance Code of Practice review
Independent review of the Life Code by Peter Kell, covering mental health, customer vulnerability, claims handling and sales practices, conducted in a way intended to support a possible application for ASIC approval.		 Council of Australian Life Insurers Under review Final report 30 June 2026 (85 recommendations); industry response and code changes to follow Insurance Life Code review
Statutory review of corporate and tax whistleblower laws
Review of whether the whistleblower protections work in practice, including whether disclosures to regulators other than ASIC and APRA should be protected.		 Treasury Under review Consultation ran 2 June to 29 July 2026; report to government to follow All sectors Treasury: review of tax and corporate whistleblowing
Super performance test review
Options to strengthen the annual performance test, including treatment of assets without a suitable market benchmark.		 Treasury Under review Consultation ran 7 May to 19 June 2026; outcome pending Super Treasury consultation
Insurance Climate Vulnerability Assessment
Modelled home insurance to 2050 with five large insurers: under both scenarios the share of homes without adequate insurance rose from about one in seven to about one in four.		 APRA Report or guidance Published March 2026 Insurance APRA: Mind the Gap
Letter to industry on artificial intelligence
APRA called for a step-change in AI-related risk management, with expectations on cyber and information security, governance, supplier risk, and change management and assurance, including for AI agents.		 APRA Report or guidance Published 30 April 2026 Banking, Insurance, Super APRA: step-change in AI-related risk management
System Risk Stress Test results
APRA's first system-wide stress test of the four major banks and six large super funds. All withstood a severe liquidity shock; APRA flagged concentration, shared service providers and similar behavioural assumptions as amplifiers.		 APRA Report or guidance Published 30 June 2026; bank liquidity changes to be consulted on from the second half of 2026 Banking, Super APRA: System Risk Stress Test findings
2026–27 supervisory priorities
Sector priorities for banking, super, general and life insurance and markets. For super: a new review of trustees' oversight of advice fee deductions, the next phase of the member services review, and retirement income covenant performance. Also AI, scams and cyber resilience.		 ASIC Report or guidance Published 30 September 2026 Super, Banking, Insurance, AFS licensees ASIC: 26-232MR
RG 181 Managing conflicts of interest (reissued)
ASIC's first major update to its conflicts guidance for AFS licensees since 2004, signalling higher expectations.		 ASIC Report or guidance Reissued 16 December 2025 AFS licensees, Super, Banking, Insurance, Managed funds ASIC: RG 181
Report 820: private credit surveillance
Found weaker practices in conflicts, fees and margins, valuation and transparency, and terminology across private credit funds.		 ASIC Report or guidance Published 5 November 2025; surveillance continuing in 2026 Managed funds ASIC: REP 820
Report 833: platform trustees' monitoring of risks to retirement savings
Review of six platform trustees found persistent gaps in monitoring advice fee deductions, unusual fees and investments, and high-risk switching.		 ASIC Report or guidance Published 29 June 2026 Super ASIC: 26-135MR
Report 839: review of the first sustainability reports
Review of 40 Group 1 reports: more and more comparable climate information than voluntary reporting, but room to improve forward-looking disclosures; some disclaimers inconsistent with the law's purpose.		 ASIC Report or guidance Published 21 September 2026 Listed entities, Super, Managed funds, Banking, Insurance ASIC: sustainability reporting

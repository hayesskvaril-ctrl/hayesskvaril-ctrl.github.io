Sector deep-dives

The same risk, compliance and governance ideas apply across financial services and beyond, but each sector has its own licences, laws, regulators and hot spots. These pages show how it all fits together in each one.

Intermediate About 5 minutes Last reviewed: 27 September 2026 Checked against sources

Key takeaways

  • Banks, insurers and super trustees are regulated by both APRA (financial soundness) and ASIC (conduct). This is the twin peaks model.
  • Managed investment schemes are mainly regulated by ASIC under the Corporations Act, not by APRA.
  • Cross-industry rules such as CPS 230, CPS 234 and the Financial Accountability Regime apply to all APRA-regulated sectors.

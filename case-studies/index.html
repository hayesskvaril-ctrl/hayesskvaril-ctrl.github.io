How these case studies are written

Each case study uses facts only from official reports, regulator publications and court outcomes, listed at the end of the page. Each includes a timeline, what went wrong, the regulatory response, lessons, "what would you have done?" prompts and a short quiz. They describe what was found or decided at the time; they are not a comment on the organisations as they are today.

Obligations for life insurers

The 25 regimes that usually apply to life insurers, grouped by theme, with 84 plain-English obligation summaries, control objectives, evidence and notification deadlines.

25 regimes 84 obligation summaries 14 notification deadlines Last reviewed: 7 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • This page lists the regimes that typically apply to life insurers. Whether each one applies to a particular organisation depends on its licences, products, size and thresholds.
  • Each summary groups related obligations in plain English. It is a starting point for an obligations register, not a substitute for the law: always read the official source.
  • The IDs match the obligations library and the GRC model builder, so you can download the full set or build a linked model.

Life insurers also have rules specific to life insurance, such as statutory funds under the Life Insurance Act 1995, caps on advice commissions and the Life Insurance Code of Practice (see "Insurance sector rules" below). The life insurance guide explains them.

Obligations for private health insurers

The 12 regimes that usually apply to private health insurers, grouped by theme, with 40 plain-English obligation summaries, control objectives, evidence and notification deadlines.

12 regimes 40 obligation summaries 8 notification deadlines Last reviewed: 7 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • This page lists the regimes that typically apply to private health insurers. Whether each one applies to a particular organisation depends on its licences, products, size and thresholds.
  • Each summary groups related obligations in plain English. It is a starting point for an obligations register, not a substitute for the law: always read the official source.
  • The IDs match the obligations library and the GRC model builder, so you can download the full set or build a linked model.

Private health insurance is not a "financial product" under the Corporations Act, so ASIC's licensing, conduct and breach reporting regimes don't apply to health insurance products. Product, premium and community rating rules come from the Private Health Insurance Act 2007, complaints go to the Commonwealth Ombudsman (as Private Health Insurance Ombudsman), and consumer law applies. See the private health insurance guide for those rules, which aren't in the obligations library yet.

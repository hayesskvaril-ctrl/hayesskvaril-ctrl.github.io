Compliance

What Australian organisations, especially in financial services, have to comply with, and how they organise themselves to do it.

Beginner to Intermediate 7 topics Last reviewed: 27 September 2026

New to compliance?

Start with What is compliance? and the regulatory landscape map, then follow the learning path. Terms are defined in the Glossary.

Learning path

Step 1 is how compliance is organised. Step 2 is what happens when things go wrong. Steps 3–7 cover the main bodies of law that financial services compliance teams work with every day.

  1. Designing a compliance program

    Obligations registers, controls, monitoring, regulatory change and reporting.

  2. Breach and incident reporting obligations

    Who must be told what, and when: ASIC, APRA, OAIC, AUSTRAC and affected clients.

  3. Licensing basics

    AFS licences, credit licences, RSE licences and what licensees must do.

  4. Disclosure obligations

    PDSs, FSGs, SOAs, target market determinations, super fund disclosure and misleading conduct.

  5. Consumer protection

    Unfair terms, hawking, design and distribution, complaints handling, scams.

  6. Privacy law

    The Privacy Act, the Australian Privacy Principles, data breaches and recent reforms.

  7. AML/CTF fundamentals

    Money laundering, customer due diligence, reporting and the 2024–26 reforms.

GRC systems

How a complex organisation designs, builds and runs an integrated governance, risk and compliance system, and how every part of it links together.

Beginner to Advanced 10 topics Last reviewed: 1 October 2026 Checked against sources

Build your own GRC model

The GRC model builder asks a few questions about your organisation (its industry, structure, regulators and risks) and builds an illustrative GRC model: the framework documents, a three-level risk taxonomy, governance committees, the data model that links it all, a reporting calendar and an implementation roadmap. Everything stays in your browser.

Open the model builder

GRC on one page

A GRC system connects what an organisation is trying to achieve with the risks to those objectives, the obligations it must meet, the controls that manage both, and the evidence that the controls work. The diagram shows the main building blocks; the pages in this section explain each one and how they link.

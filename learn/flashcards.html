Flashcards

Learn the language and the numbers. Flip each card, then mark whether you knew it. Cards you don't know come back until you do.

Last reviewed: 28 September 2026

Tips

  • Short, frequent sessions beat one long one. Try the 20-card glossary deck a few times a week.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Space flips the card, 1 means "Again" and 2 means "Got it".
  • Deadlines and thresholds change. Each card links to the page with the detail and sources.

My learning

Your progress through the learning pathways, what to read next, and a printable record of learning. Everything is saved in this browser only.

About 2 minutes Last reviewed: 1 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Press Mark as read on any page in a pathway, or tick pages on the pathways page.
  • See your progress in every pathway and the next step in each.
  • Print a record of learning listing the pages and pathways you have completed.

How progress works

  • Pages count as read when you press Mark as read on the page, or tick them on the learning pathways page.
  • Progress is saved only in this browser. It isn't sent anywhere, and it won't follow you to another device. Clearing your browser's site data clears it.
  • The record of learning lists what you have marked as read. It is self-recorded: it is not an assessment, a qualification or a certificate. Some professional bodies accept self-directed reading as continuing professional development (CPD); check your own body's rules.

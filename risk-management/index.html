Risk management

How organisations identify, assess, control and monitor the risks to their objectives, explained with Australian examples and the relevant APRA and ASIC requirements.

Beginner to Advanced 23 topics Last reviewed: 27 September 2026

New to risk management?

Start with the What is risk management? primer, then follow the learning path below. Terms are defined in the Glossary.

Learning path

The topics build on each other. Steps 1–4 are the core toolkit; steps 5–9 apply it to specific types of risk and events.

  1. Set the boundaries: risk appetite and tolerance

    How much risk the board is prepared to take, and how that becomes measurable limits.

  2. Assess the risks

    Likelihood × consequence, qualitative and quantitative methods, heat maps and their limits.

  3. Control the risks

    How to design controls that work, and how to test that they do.

  4. Join it up: enterprise risk management

    Managing risk across the whole organisation, linked to strategy and culture.

  5. Operational risk

    People, process, systems and external events, and APRA's CPS 230.

  6. Credit, market and liquidity risk

    The core financial risks, in plain English.

  7. Incidents and breaches

    What to do when something goes wrong, and when regulators must be told.

  8. Business continuity

    Keeping critical operations running through severe disruption.

  9. Third-party and outsourcing risk

    Managing the risks of relying on service providers.

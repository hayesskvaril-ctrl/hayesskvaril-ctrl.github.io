SPS 530 Investment Governance

APRA's standard for how super trustees govern the investment of members' money: setting objectives and strategies, choosing and monitoring investments, valuing them, and managing liquidity and stress.

Intermediate About 8 minutes Last reviewed: 27 September 2026 Checked against sources
Issuer
Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA)
Status
Binding prudential standard. Current version applies from 1 January 2023, with strengthened valuation and liquidity requirements.
Applies to
RSE licensees (super trustees).
Guidance
Prudential practice guide SPG 530.

Key takeaways

  • SPS 530 requires a board-approved investment governance framework covering how investment decisions are made, monitored and reviewed.
  • Each investment option needs objectives, a strategy to achieve them, and ongoing monitoring of performance and risk.
  • Trustees must carry out due diligence before investing, and monitor investments and managers afterwards.
  • Valuation governance and liquidity management, including a comprehensive stress-testing program, are core requirements.
  • Unlisted assets, such as property, infrastructure and private credit, raise the stakes for valuation and liquidity.

What SPS 530 requires

AreaIn plain English
Investment governance frameworkBoard-approved structures, policies and processes for making and monitoring investment decisions, reviewed regularly.
Investment objectives and strategyClear return and risk objectives for each investment option, and a strategy to achieve them that considers diversification, liquidity, risk and costs.
Due diligence and selectionA documented process for choosing investments and managers, proportionate to their risk and complexity.
MonitoringOngoing monitoring of performance against objectives, of investment managers and of risks, with action when things go off track.
ValuationA valuation governance framework so assets, especially unlisted ones, are valued fairly and consistently, including revaluation when conditions change significantly.
LiquidityA liquidity management plan so the fund can pay benefits, rollovers and switches, including in stress.
Stress testingA board-approved investment stress-testing program with adverse scenarios for each option's strategy.

Why valuation and liquidity matter so much

Super funds hold growing amounts of unlisted assets. Their values aren't set by a market every day, so stale or inconsistent valuations can transfer wealth between members: those leaving at an overstated value gain at the expense of those who stay. And because members can switch and roll over their money at short notice, funds need enough liquid assets to pay them without forced selling. The 2020 COVID-19 early release of super and market volatility were a real-world test of both.

Practical points

  • Revalue when markets move, not just on a fixed schedule. Define triggers for off-cycle revaluations.
  • Test liquidity under combined stresses, such as a market fall plus high switching plus early release.
  • Watch unit pricing. Valuation errors flow directly into unit prices and member balances, and may require remediation.
  • Link to member outcomes. Investment performance is central to the annual outcomes assessment under SPS 515 and to the performance test.

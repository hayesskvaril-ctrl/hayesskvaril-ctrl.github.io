CPS 510 Governance and CPS 520 Fit and Proper (and SPS equivalents)

APRA's core rules on how boards are structured and run, and on making sure the people in key roles have the competence, character and integrity to hold them.

Intermediate About 9 minutes Last reviewed: 27 September 2026 Checked against sources
Issuer
Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA)
Status
Binding prudential standards. APRA consulted in 2026 on a single, consolidated cross-industry governance standard that would replace these standards (see below).
Applies to
CPS 510 and CPS 520: ADIs and insurers. SPS 510 and SPS 520: RSE licensees. SPS 521 covers conflicts of interest for super trustees.
Guidance
Prudential practice guides including SPG 510 and SPG 520.

Key takeaways

  • The governance standards set minimum requirements for board size, independence, committees, board renewal and performance review.
  • The fit and proper standards require entities to assess the competence, character, diligence, honesty and integrity of "responsible persons", such as directors and senior managers, before appointment and at least annually.
  • Entities must have a fit and proper policy, keep records of assessments, and notify APRA if someone is assessed as not fit and proper.
  • APRA has proposed replacing these standards with one cross-industry governance standard, with proposals such as tenure limits for directors.

Governance: what the standards cover

AreaTypical requirements (summarised)
Board compositionMinimum board size; for banks and insurers, a majority of independent directors and an independent chair.
Board committeesA board audit committee and a remuneration committee (with a risk committee required under CPS 220 for many entities), with rules on membership and independence.
Board renewal and performancePolicies on director nomination, appointment, tenure and renewal, and regular assessment of the board's performance.
Internal and external auditAn independent internal audit function with access to the audit committee, and arrangements for the external auditor. See Internal audit.
Conflicts (super)SPS 521 requires RSE licensees to identify and manage conflicts of interest and duty.

Fit and proper: what the standards cover

  1. Identify responsible persons

    Directors, senior managers, the auditor and actuary (where relevant) and others in key roles.

  2. Set criteria

    Competence (skills, knowledge and experience for the role) and propriety (honesty, integrity, character and diligence, including checks such as criminal history and bankruptcy).

  3. Assess

    Before appointment and at least annually, with documented evidence.

  4. Act and notify

    Take action if someone isn't fit and proper, and notify APRA within 10 business days of such an assessment.

Accountable persons under the Financial Accountability Regime must also meet FAR's own requirements.

Proposed changes

In 2026 APRA consulted on a single cross-industry governance standard that would replace CPS 510, SPS 510, CPS 520, SPS 520 and SPS 521. Proposals included a default 12-year tenure limit for non-executive directors and a minimum of five directors for super trustee boards. The final standard and start dates are not yet settled. See What APRA's proposed governance standard would change.

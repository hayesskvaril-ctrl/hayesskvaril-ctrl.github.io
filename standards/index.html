Regulatory and standards library

One plain-English explainer per instrument, focused on what it actually requires you to do, with links to the official source.

Intermediate Last reviewed: 27 September 2026

Always check the official version

Standards and guides are updated regularly. Each explainer shows the version and date we checked, but the official text on the regulator's or publisher's website is what counts. ISO and COSO documents are copyrighted, so we describe them in our own words only.

How the pieces fit

TypeBinding?Examples
APRA prudential standards (CPS, SPS, APS, GPS, LPS, HPS)Yes: legally binding on regulated entitiesCPS 230, CPS 220, CPS 234
APRA prudential practice guides (CPG, SPG…)No: guidance on APRA's expectations and better practiceCPG 230, CPG 234
ASIC regulatory guides (RG)Mostly guidance; some paragraphs are made enforceable by legislative instrument (e.g. parts of RG 271)RG 78, RG 97, RG 259, RG 271, RG 274, RG 277
International standards and frameworksVoluntary (unless required by contract); some are certifiableISO 31000, ISO/IEC 27001, COSO

APRA's prefixes: C = cross-industry, A = ADIs (banks), G = general insurers, L = life insurers, H = private health insurers, S = superannuation.

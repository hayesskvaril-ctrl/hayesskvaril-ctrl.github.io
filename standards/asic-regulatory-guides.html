Key ASIC regulatory guides

ASIC's regulatory guides explain how ASIC reads the law and what it expects. This page maps the ones financial services compliance teams use most, with detailed explainers for four of them.

Beginner About 8 minutes Last reviewed: 27 September 2026 Checked against sources

Key takeaways

  • Regulatory guides (RGs) are ASIC's published guidance. They are generally not law, but they show how ASIC interprets the law and what it will look for.
  • Some RG paragraphs are made legally enforceable by ASIC legislative instruments. For example, the enforceable paragraphs of RG 271 on complaints handling.
  • RGs are numbered, updated from time to time, and sometimes withdrawn. Always check the current version on ASIC's website.

ISO 37301 Compliance management systems

The international standard for building a compliance management system, with Australian roots.

Intermediate About 7 minutes Last reviewed: 27 September 2026
Publisher
International Organization for Standardization (ISO)
Current edition
ISO 37301:2021 (with a 2024 amendment on climate change considerations).
Status
Voluntary. Certifiable, unlike its predecessor ISO 19600, which only gave guidance.
History
Replaced ISO 19600:2014, which drew on the Australian standard AS 3806:2006 on compliance programs.

Key takeaways

  • ISO 37301 sets out requirements for a compliance management system: how an organisation identifies its compliance obligations and makes sure it meets them.
  • It places heavy weight on leadership and culture, including a compliance function with independence, authority and access to the governing body.
  • The operational core is identifying obligations, assessing compliance risks, putting controls in place, and handling concerns and non-compliance.
  • It has Australian roots: its predecessor built on AS 3806, a pioneering Australian compliance standard.
  • Regulators don't require certification, but the standard is a useful benchmark for designing a compliance program.

Key features

FeatureIn plain English
Compliance obligationsA process for identifying requirements (laws, regulations, licence conditions) and voluntary commitments (codes, contracts), and keeping them up to date. See obligations register.
Compliance risk assessmentAssessing the risk of failing to meet each obligation and prioritising controls.
Compliance cultureLeaders demonstrating commitment, consistent consequences, and compliance built into how people work and are rewarded.
The compliance functionA function with the independence, authority, resources and direct access to the governing body needed to do its job.
Raising concerns and investigationsChannels for reporting suspected non-compliance, and processes to investigate it.
Monitoring and improvementIndicators, reporting, audits and management reviews that drive improvement.

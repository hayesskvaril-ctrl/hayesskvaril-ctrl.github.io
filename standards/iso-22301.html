ISO 22301 Business continuity management systems

The international standard for setting up and running a business continuity management system that can be certified.

Intermediate About 14 minutes Last reviewed: 8 October 2026 Checked against sources
Publisher
International Organization for Standardization (ISO)
Current edition
ISO 22301:2019, with a 2024 amendment adding climate change considerations (as for other ISO management system standards).
Status
Voluntary. Certifiable by accredited certification bodies.
Related
ISO 22313 gives guidance on using ISO 22301.

Key takeaways

  • ISO 22301 sets requirements for a business continuity management system (BCMS): the policies, processes and people that keep critical activities running through disruption.
  • Its core operational steps are business impact analysis, risk assessment, continuity strategies and solutions, plans and procedures, and exercising and testing.
  • It follows the common structure of ISO management system standards, so it fits alongside ISO/IEC 27001 and others.
  • It doesn't replace CPS 230, but many APRA-regulated entities use it as a reference framework for their business continuity programs.

What clause 8 (operation) covers

ElementIn plain English
Business impact analysisIdentify the activities that deliver key products and services, how quickly disruption causes unacceptable impact, and what resources they depend on. See Business continuity.
Risk assessmentAssess the risks of disruption to those activities.
Strategies and solutionsChoose ways to protect, stabilise, continue, resume and recover activities within the required timeframes.
Plans and proceduresDocumented response structure, incident response, communication and recovery plans.
Exercising and testingA program of exercises to check plans work and people know their roles.
EvaluationAssess whether the business continuity documentation and capabilities are still fit for purpose.

ISO 22301 and CPS 230

CPS 230 uses its own concepts, such as critical operations and board-approved tolerance levels, and adds requirements about service providers and notifying APRA that ISO 22301 doesn't include. An ISO 22301-aligned program provides a strong foundation, but entities still need to map it to CPS 230's specific requirements. See Setting CPS 230 tolerance levels.

Building a business continuity management system

StepWhat it involves
Business impact analysisIdentify activities, how quickly they must be restored, and what they depend on (people, systems, sites, suppliers).
Risk assessmentWhat could disrupt those activities, and how likely and severe it would be.
Strategies and solutionsHow each activity would continue or recover, such as alternate sites, manual workarounds or a second supplier.
Plans and proceduresResponse, communication and recovery plans that people can use under pressure.
Exercising and testingTabletop and live exercises, with lessons fed back into the plans.
Evaluation and improvementInternal audit, management review and corrective action.

The 2024 climate amendment

In February 2024 ISO amended many management system standards, including this one, to say that an organisation must consider whether climate change is a relevant issue for its management system, and that interested parties may have climate-related requirements. Certification bodies now check how that decision was made.

Common mistakes

  • Plans that assume key suppliers will be fine in a widespread disruption.
  • Recovery times set without asking what customers or members can tolerate.
  • Exercises that only test IT recovery, not people, communication and decision-making.

Frequently asked questions

Does ISO 22301 certification satisfy CPS 230?

No. They overlap a lot, but CPS 230 has specific requirements, such as critical operations, board-approved tolerance levels, APRA notification and service provider management. See CPS 230.

What's new

New pages, notable updates and site improvements, newest first.

Beginner Last updated: 27 September 2026

For regulatory news, see News and the Regulatory changes tracker. Every page is reviewed at least once a year; its "Last reviewed" date and next review date are shown at the bottom of the page. Pages checked by the site's subject-matter expert show an Expert reviewed badge. Read more about how content is checked.

27 September 2026

Updates

  • Regulatory and standards library: Added 15 new explainers, including CPS 511, SPS 515, SPS 530, CPS 190 and CPS 900, RG 181, RG 270, ISO 22301, ISO 37301, ISO/IEC 42001 and the Essential Eight.
  • Learning pathways: Added the new risk, governance, cyber and standards pages to the learning pathways.
  • AI governance: Added APRA's April 2026 letter to industry on AI risk management.
  • Regulatory changes tracker: Added mandatory climate reporting, further financial advice reforms, the proposed modern slavery offence and the foreign bribery failure to prevent offence.
  • Learning pathways: Added an Advanced stage to the risk, compliance, governance and superannuation pathways.
  • Start here: Added a "Go deeper" route through the seven new Advanced topics.
  • Glossary: Added VaR, expected shortfall, reverse stress testing, ICAAP, diversification benefit and tolerable deviation rate.
  • New look: the whole site moved to a dark, futuristic design, with fonts hosted on the site itself so no third-party requests are made.
  • Financial Accountability Regime (FAR): Added the regulators' proposed simplifications to the Financial Accountability Regime.
  • Consumer protection: Updated the Scams Prevention Framework timetable.
  • Glossary: Added 34 terms, including ADI, responsible entity, CET1, hawking, significant dealing and SMSF, and linked them from the pages that use them.
  • Added link previews for sharing, a browser-tab icon, a sitemap and a friendly page-not-found page, and made the faintest text easier to read.
  • Yearly review system: every page now shows when its next review is due, and pages checked by the site's subject-matter expert will carry an "Expert reviewed" badge.
